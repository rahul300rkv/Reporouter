Contact Us

Found a bug, spotted an error in a guide, or want to suggest an improvement? Use the contact form or the public repository issue tracker.

Email reporouter@rediffmail.com or use the form below to report reproducible software bugs. Response times vary; we cannot promise an individual reply to every submission.

Helpful details to include

  • For bugs: the repository link you pasted, your browser and device, and what you expected to happen.
  • For corrections: the guide's title and the sentence you think is wrong.

We describe how we handle reported errors in our editorial and corrections policy.

We cannot give support for the third-party tools themselves (GitMCP, DeepWiki, Gitingest, UIthub). Please contact those services directly for account or availability issues.
