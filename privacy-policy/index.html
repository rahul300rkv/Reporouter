Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Repo Router (“we”, “us”) handles when you use reporouter.netlify.app. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. What stays in your browser

The URL conversion is performed by JavaScript running on your device. The repository links you type are not sent to our servers. The tool stores a short list of recently used repositories in your browser's local storage so you can reuse them. This list never leaves your device, and you can remove it with the “clear” button or by clearing your browser data.

2. Requests made from your browser

To work, the page may contact other services directly from your browser, and those services can see your IP address and standard request details:

  • GitHub API (api.github.com), to display repository details such as stars, language and last update.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), to load typefaces.
  • cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com), to load the QR code library.
  • Shields.io, only if you use the generated README badge, because the badge image is hosted there.
  • Destination sites (GitMCP, DeepWiki, Gitingest, UIthub) when you choose to open them. Their own privacy policies apply from that point.

3. Information you send us

If you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message. We use them only to reply and to improve the site, and we do not sell them. Form submissions are handled by our hosting provider, Netlify.

4. Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Netlify. Like most hosts, Netlify may process technical data such as IP addresses and browser type to deliver and secure the service.

5. Cookies and advertising

Repo Router itself does not set tracking cookies. We use, or may use, third-party advertising, including Google AdSense, to help pay for the site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info.

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before using cookies for personalized advertising.

6. Analytics

We do not currently run analytics that identify individual visitors. If that changes, we will update this page.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold, such as a contact message you sent. Email Mail and we will respond.

8. Children

Repo Router is a developer tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. External links

Our pages link to websites we do not control. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to hello@reporouter.netlify.app or use the contact page.
